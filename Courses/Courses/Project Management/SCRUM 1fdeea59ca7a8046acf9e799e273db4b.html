SCRUM

  • foundamental is not waste resources, deliver as soon as possible

being rapid is not enough → we need to deliver good services, but without wastes

  • need to respond to changes

SCRUM’s goal is to deliver as much quality software as possible within a series, of short time intervals called Sprints that typically last about a month.

SCRUM is not only an "iterative and incremental" development method but also an "adaptive" software development method

There is not PM, the team will self organize → small teams (10 people)

Product owner will take the product goal of the stakeholders and create the requirements for the developers

  • prioritize some works
  • accept or reject the results of a sprint → authority
  • plans the release of product with customer and developers
  • maximize ROI of the product

Developers needs to be cross-functional (7 +/- 2)

  • not too much specialized, better generalists

Scrum Master

  • Can keep an impediment backlog and report on the status of the impediments at the
    Daily Scrum
  • helps PO in preparing sprint meetings
  • helps dev team in praparing review meeting
  • prepare and facilitate daily scrum
  • coaches the dev team to become more effective
  • is a servant leader → it coaches, not mandate

Product Vision

  • Start with the desired end-state, then add intent
  • should be concise and engaging

Product BackLog

  • An emergent, ordered list of what is needed to improve the product
  • dynamic, ordered and reordered, reprioritize
  • The Given-When-Then format is used to describe acceptance criteria — that are specific conditions that a User Story must meet for the product owner to accept it as done.
  • typically the oreder is: Build the essentials first, validate them, then layer on the extras while focusing on value and risk reduction.
  • The Product Owner (PO) doesn’t need to write all the User Stories → also dev team is important in this phase, eg. via workshops

Sprint Planning Meeting

  • SM facilitate this meeting
  • PO present the backlog items in priority and clarify the meanings, in the form of user stories
  • dev team estimates the effort

It is time-boxed to eight hours for a one month Sprint. For shorter Sprints the event is usually
shorter

  • more items together to one Sprint

Sprint Backlog

  • list of items that are committed to be accomplished during the sprint, taken out form the product backlog
  • broken down into tasks
  • every team member can modify the sprint backlog
  • work is never assigned but pulled out from the backlog
  • items not completed in a sprint are moved to the product backlog, not to the next sprint!

Sprints

1 to 4-week consecutive time period where the team builds the Product Backlog items they committed to during Sprint Planning

  • No changes are allowed during the Sprint
  • The end of the Sprint is fixed
  • “Done” is determined by the conditions of satisfaction identified by the Product Owner and Team
  • Potentially shippable is a portion of product functionality completely developed and tested by the team during a Sprint
    • not equal to shippable

Only the Product Owner can cancel the Sprint

Review Meeting

  • SM moderates
  • team present the software developed → Showcase their accompliments
  • PO accepts or not, based on the acceptance criteria, that was agreed in the sprint planning meeting
  • Feedback goes into Product Backlog to be developed into user stories for future iterations

Length: 4 hours for a monthly sprint; it is usually shorter for shorter sprints

A lot of definition of DONE:

  1. peer reviewed
  1. tested agaist current version
  1. unit tests passed

Daily Scrum

The Daily Scrum is often a Stand-Up meeting attended by the developers team and is time-boxed to 15 minutes

Three questions:

  1. What did you complete since the last daily Scrum?
  1. What impediments got on your way?
  1. What are you going to complete before the next daily Scrum?

Scrum Overview

  • Opportunity for the Scrum team to reflect on methods and team work
  • Timeboxed to 3-hours for one-month Sprint
  • Occurs after the sprint review
