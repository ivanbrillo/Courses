Distribution

Making products and services available to the customers

  • temporal and spacial asymmetry between production and consumption
  • need to coordinate the quantity, the space and the time
  • Eg software companies don’t need any intermidary B2B (business to business)
  • B2C quite rare (business to customer)

  • Wholesaler → very large wherehouse near key logistic points (eg. train), to store high volume of items to sell to the retailers
    • Intended to supply the retailer, not the final customer → retailer cannot store huge number of products, but need refill periodically
  • Agent → independent intermediary (partita IVA), that premotes the sale of my product (it doesn’t buy my products)
    • Wholesaler and retailer buy the product and resell it

Gaining access to distribution outlets is not necessarily easy.

  • difficult for achiving the prime positions

Gillete does adv in order to achive higher bargaining power (potere contrattuale) to achive better shelfs positions

  • Distributors purchase products from producers and resell to customers

Channel strategy decisions involve the selection of:

  1. Decide the most effective distribution channels → taking care where is the industry, the market size, perishability, ….
  1. the most appropriate level of distribution intensity
    1. intensive distribution that saturate the market (for the customer they are everywhere)
    1. selective distribution (limited number of outlets eg. for a book) → typically less frequent purchase and higher price
    1. exclusive distribution (for luxary goods)
  1. the degree of channel integration
    • convential marketing channels → producer has no control over channel intermediaris
    • vertiacal marketing systems → via the brand power exercise considerable power over intermediaries
      • Producer can decide the final price of the product (se troppo scontanto le persone iniziano a pensare che non valga così tanto dopo la promozione)
      • franchising → agreement for the vertaical marketing system, following very strict rules (eg. McDonalds)
