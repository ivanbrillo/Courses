NLP Techiniques

  1. Tokenization: Splitting text into individual words, phrases, or sentences.
  1. Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging: Assigning parts of speech (e.g., nouns, verbs) to each word in a sentence.
  1. Named Entity Recognition (NER): Identifying and classifying entities (e.g., names, dates, locations) in text.
  1. Lemmatization: Reducing words to their base or root form.
  1. Stemming: Reducing words to their root forms by removing suffixes.
  1. Dependency Parsing: Analyzing the grammatical structure of a sentence and establishing relationships between words.
  1. Word Embeddings: Representing words in a continuous vector space (e.g. BoW, Word2Vec, GloVe, BERT, GPT).

TM Techniques

  1. Text Classification: Assigning predefined categories to text (e.g., spam detection, sentiment analysis, topic labeling). Techniques: Naive Bayes, Support Vector Machines (SVM), Random Forests
  1. Sentiment Analysis: Determining the emotional tone (positive, negative, neutral) of text.
  1. Text Clustering: Grouping similar documents together based on their content. K-Means, Hierarchical Clustering.
  1. Topic Modeling: Identifying hidden themes or topics within a set of texts. Techniques: Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA), Non-Negative Matrix Factorization (NMF). -
  1. Association Rule Mining: Discovering interesting relations between variables in large datasets (e.g., market basket analysis).
  1. Keyword Extraction: Identifying the most important words or phrases in a text.
  1. Co-occurrence Network Analysis: Analyzing the co-occurrence of words or entities within a text to understand relationships.
  1. Document Similarity: Measuring the similarity between texts. Techniques: Cosine Similarity, Jaccard Similarity

GenLLM Techniques

Almost all the previous and:

  1. Language Modeling: Predicting the next word in a sequence of words. Generating text that continues a given prompt, producing contextually relevant and coherent sentences.
  1. Text Summarization: Creating a condensed version of a text while retaining key information
  1. Machine Translation: Translating text from one language to another

Measuring the Output

  1. Numerical:
    • Sentiment Score: Quantifies the overall sentiment of the text, typically on a scale from negative to positive.
    • Classification Probability: The likelihood that a document belongs to a particular category or class.
    • Topic Probability: The likelihood that a document pertains to a specific topic.
    • Number of Words Belonging to a Lexicon: Counts the occurrence of words from a predefined lexicon within the document.
  1. Categorical:
    • Classification Output: The assigned category or class of the document, such as spam/not spam, topic
    labels, etc.
    • Topic: The dominant topic or themes identified in the document.
    • Yes/No Answer Given by Generative LLMs: Binary outputs generated by language models in response to
    queries.
    • Named Entities: Entities such as persons, organizations, locations, identified in the text.

Numerical:
• Importance of a Word in a Document: Metrics like TF-IDF score indicating the significance of a word
within a document relative to a corpus.
• Level of Abstraction of a Word: A score indicating how abstract or concrete a word is, often derived from
lexical databases.
• Complexity of a Word: Measured by factors such as syllable count, word length, or frequency of use in
the language.
• Word Embedding Vector: A high-dimensional vector representation of a word used in NLP models,
capturing semantic relationships.
Categorical:
• Word Belongs to a Lexicon (Y/N): Indicates whether the word is part of a predefined lexicon or dictionary.
• Part of Speech: Classification of the word as noun, verb, adjective, etc.
• Named Entity Recognition: Identification of the word as a named entity like person, organization, location,
etc.
• Domain-Specific Classification: Identification of the word as belonging to a specific domain, such as
medical, legal, technical.
